## 2. Competitor profiles

### 2.1 Fresha — https://www.fresha.com/pricing
**Saudi pricing (raw HTML from a Saudi IP, 2026-10-03):**
- Independent (1 team member): **SAR 149.95 / month**, 7-day trial. Team & Enterprise (20+): **custom rates — NOT PUBLICLY DISCLOSED in KSA**.
- New clients from the Fresha marketplace: **50 % one-time commission, minimum SAR 10 per client**; returning clients free.
- Online payments: **4.90 % + SAR 0.75** per transaction.
- Automated text SAR 0.35; **WhatsApp SAR 0.15–0.30**; marketing text SAR 0.40; marketing email SAR 0.07 (after free allowances).
- Add-ons: Google Rating Boost SAR 35.95/location; Team Connect SAR 25.95/member; Insights SAR 319.95/location; Xero SAR 49.95; Data Connector SAR 1,100/location; Smart Website SAR 129.95; Client Loyalty custom.
- No minimum commitment.

**US comparison (same page, US crawler):** Independent $19.95; Team **$14.95 per bookable team member**; new clients **20 %, min $6**; online 2.79 % + $0.20; **AI Concierge add-on $99.95/location** (not listed on the Saudi page).

**Features (plan comparison):** calendar, waitlists, POS, client management, consultation forms, reviews, retail & inventory, online store, quotes & invoices, reporting, marketing, deals, gift cards, packages, memberships, group bookings, processing & finishing times, resources, patch tests; free direct booking links, Facebook and Instagram booking; upfront payments and no-show/cancellation fees. Team plan adds login permissions, commissions, wages, pay runs, timesheets and per-member service pricing.

**Lesson for PRIMORA:** Fresha's Saudi new-client fee is **2.5× its US rate**. A Saudi salon that gets 60 new marketplace clients a month at SAR 150 pays SAR 4,500 in acquisition fees alone. That is the single clearest commercial opening in this research.

### 2.2 Glamera — https://business.glamera.com/en/ · /en/pricing (raw HTML, Saudi IP)
- Products: **Business** (appointments, POS, inventory, staff, accounting, marketing, reports, multi-branch) · **Pro** (website builder with online booking, SMS & WhatsApp, online payments, **custom domains**) · **One** (owner/staff mobile app) · **Pay** (payment links, invoices, refunds) · **Kiosk** (self check-in, booking, payment, queue).
- **ZATCA e-invoicing Phase 1 & 2**, commission tracking, loyalty, accounting.
- **Pricing (SAR/month):** Starter **125** (≤3 users) · Basic **225** (≤10 users) · Advanced **325** (≤20 users, accounting, account manager) · **+SAR 10 per extra employee** · up to 20 % off annually · free trial. Processing fees **NOT PUBLICLY DISCLOSED**.
- Scale claims: 3,500+ businesses, 10+ countries, 50M+ bookings, 20M+ invoices (site); SAR 3B GMV with 4,000+ partners (Glamera LinkedIn, 2025-12-21); SAR 4B+ processed, 4,500+ providers, Bookr acquisition (*third-party*, MENA Startup Digest, 2026-01-27); fintech license (*third-party*, Arab News, 2023-08-23).

**Lesson:** Glamera already owns the "Saudi salon operating system + ZATCA" position at a low flat price. PRIMORA cannot win by being a cheaper Glamera. It can win where Glamera is weak: **consumer demand generation** and a **premium, AI/WhatsApp-native customer experience**.

### 2.3 SPOT by Slotex — https://slotex.sa/ · https://www.slotex.sa/terms (terms updated 2026-03-22)
- Booking link "from one link" for **Instagram and WhatsApp**, "60 seconds to book", 24/7 self-booking.
- **Arabic SMS and WhatsApp reminders**; customer confirms or reschedules with one tap.
- Calendar (week view), walk-ins, block time, staff, revenue, new clients, chair occupancy; **multi-branch**; **VAT-ready reports**.
- Self-reported pilot (12 salons, Riyadh & Jeddah, Jan–Jun 2026): no-shows 18 % → under 6 %.
- Pricing: **custom per salon** (branches × team size); free 30-minute setup; no long-term contract; WhatsApp-led sales.
- **Terms — business logic:** intermediary; collects payment **on behalf of the merchant via a licensed Saudi processor** (cards + mada); merchant sets cancellation window, late-cancel and no-show fees **up to 100 %**, deposits up to 100 % and possibly non-refundable; tips paid 100 % to merchant less processing; promo codes at merchant discretion; customers suspended for patterns of cancellations/no-shows/chargebacks; liability capped at 12 months of fees.

**Lesson:** SPOT shows what Saudi salon owners respond to — a booking link in Instagram bio, WhatsApp reminders in Arabic, and someone on WhatsApp who sets it up for them.

### 2.4 Glamiva — https://glamiva.sa/
- Two connected apps: **Glamiva Business** and **Glamiva Customers**; copy is written to women.
- Verticals beyond beauty: massage centers, beauty clinics, **women's clubs/gyms, party coordinators, abaya and dress shops**.
- Business: multi-branch, employees and schedules, prices per branch and season, **in-app ad banners**, **daily "stories"**, time-limited offers with live uptake.
- Customer: **nearest providers on a map**, **verified ratings for the provider and each employee**, stories feed, instant booking.
- Site terms: booking is a commitment between customer and provider; **each provider sets its own cancellation and refund policy**, shown before confirmation — no platform-held funds.
- Claims +500 providers, +50K active customers. Contact via a Gmail address and a mobile WhatsApp number (early-stage signal). Pricing **NOT PUBLICLY DISCLOSED**.

## 3. User-flow comparison

### 3.1 Customer booking flow

| Step | PRIMORA today | Strongest observed pattern |
|---|---|---|
| Acquisition | Landing / `/services` only; **not indexable by Google** | Booking link in Instagram bio / WhatsApp (SPOT, Booksy, Fresha); QR (Booksy); Google booking (SQUIRE) |
| Search | Text + category + gender + price | Map "nearest to you" (Glamiva); stories (Glamiva) |
| Provider | `/shop/[id]` | Portfolio (Booksy), per-employee ratings (Glamiva) |
| Service | Single service | Combo / multi-service (Booksy, BeautyBook across salons) |
| Professional | **Must pick a person** | "Any available" + specific person |
| Time | Real slots (15-min grid) | Same, plus waitlist when full (Booksy, Fresha) |
| Payment | Tap deposit | Card on file + deposit (StyleSeat); hold-then-capture after satisfaction (Salon Station) |
| Confirmation | Receipt page + ICS | WhatsApp confirmation (SPOT) |
| Reminder | **None** | Arabic WhatsApp reminder with one-tap confirm/reschedule (SPOT) |
| Appointment | Owner marks complete | — |
| Review | Verified, completed bookings only ✅ | Verified + per-employee (Glamiva) |
| Rebook | **None** | Automated rebooking reminders (StyleSeat, Zenoti) |

### 3.2 Business onboarding

| Step | PRIMORA | Observed patterns |
|---|---|---|
| Sign-up | **Dead end** — no business account can be created | Self-serve trial: Fresha 7 days, Booksy 14 days (no card), Glamera free trial |
| Assisted setup | None | SPOT: free 30-minute setup booked over WhatsApp, reply within 2 h |
| Verification | trade-license URL only | Tap: KYC by the PSP before payouts; Wathq API can verify a Commercial Registration for SAR 2–12 per call |
| Services / staff / schedule | Exist once a provider row exists | — |
| Payments | Tap checkout; payouts manual | Tap Marketplace: sub-merchant KYC + automatic payout cycle |
| Marketplace activation | Implicit | Opt-in marketplace with transparent new-client fee (Fresha, StyleSeat, Booksy Boost) |

### 3.3 Cancellation flow

| Step | PRIMORA | Observed patterns |
|---|---|---|
| Policy set by | Nobody (Terms say 24 h / 50 %, code enforces nothing) | **Merchant**, displayed before booking (SPOT, Glamiva) |
| Fee | None | Up to 100 % (SPOT), deposits (Booksy, StyleSeat) |
| Refund | Broken function | Per policy; Salon Station captures only after satisfaction |
| Slot reopening | Yes (status leaves the constraint) | Plus waitlist notification (Booksy) |
| Provider notified | No | Yes |

### 3.4 Waitlist flow
PRIMORA: **not implemented**. Booksy: customer joins for a time frame → opening occurs → automatic notification → customer books. Fresha lists waitlists in every plan.

## 4. Competitive / differentiating features — which ones actually create advantage

| Feature | Who has it | Creates advantage? | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Real-time availability + instant booking | Most | **Table stakes** | Absence loses; presence does not win |
| Booking link / QR / Instagram / WhatsApp entry | Fresha, Booksy, SPOT, SQUIRE | **Yes, for supply acquisition** | Lets providers bring their own clients — the cheapest liquidity |
| Commission only on new marketplace clients | Fresha, StyleSeat, Treatwell, Booksy | **Yes** | Removes the incentive to move clients off-platform |
| Arabic WhatsApp reminders + one-tap confirm | SPOT | **Yes in KSA** | Saudi customers live in WhatsApp; directly cuts no-shows |
| Per-employee ratings & portfolio | Glamiva, Booksy | **Yes** | Customers book a person, not a building; employees bring followers |
| Map / nearest | Glamiva | Moderate | Useful in dense districts; weak without supply density |
| Stories / daily work feed | Glamiva | Moderate | Instagram-native discovery; needs moderation |
| Deposits / card on file / no-show fees | StyleSeat, Booksy, SPOT, Fresha | **Yes** | Protects provider revenue — a reason to stay |
| Waitlist auto-fill | Booksy, Fresha | Moderate | Recovers cancelled revenue |
| AI receptionist / concierge | Zenoti, Booksy, Mindbody, SQUIRE, Fresha (US) | **Yes later** | Saudi salons miss calls/WhatsApps; Arabic AI is under-served |
| Dynamic / smart pricing | StyleSeat | Low (now) | Needs volume; risks trust |
| ZATCA Phase 2 e-invoicing | Glamera | **Yes until Feb 2027** | Wave 25 forces most VAT-registered salons to integrate |
| Website / custom domain / branded app | Glamera Pro, Vagaro, SQUIRE, Salonist | Moderate | Business-owned brand; good upsell |
| Prayer-time aware scheduling | **PRIMORA only (observed)** | **Yes in KSA** | Locally obvious, globally absent |
| Family / dependent booking | **PRIMORA** (observed) | **Yes in KSA** | Mothers booking for daughters, fathers for sons |

## 5. Customer-ownership models observed

| Model | Examples | Customer relationship |
|---|---|---|
| Marketplace-first | Treatwell, Fresha marketplace, StyleSeat NCC | Platform finds the client, charges once, then the client "belongs" to the provider |
| Business-owned | SQUIRE ("never shown to other businesses"), Salonist white-label, Glamera Pro | Provider's brand, provider's clients |
| Hybrid | Fresha (free direct links + paid marketplace), Booksy (profile link/QR + Boost) | Both, priced differently by **source** |

The hybrid model — **price by acquisition source, not by booking** — is what the strongest players converge on. PRIMORA currently does the opposite: it charges the same commission on every booking regardless of who brought the client (see report 14).
