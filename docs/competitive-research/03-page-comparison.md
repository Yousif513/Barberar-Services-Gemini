## 4. What the matrix says

1. **PRIMORA's customer funnel is complete enough to transact** — search → profile → employee → slot → deposit → confirmation — and it has two screens nobody else showed: **family/dependent booking** and **prayer-aware availability**.
2. **The gaps cluster after the booking** (reminders, reschedule, waitlist, rebooking, loyalty) **and before it on the supply side** (self-serve onboarding, booking link/QR, verification). Those are exactly the screens competitors use to acquire and retain providers.
3. **Three PRIMORA screens are broken against a real database** (notifications, promotions, CRM notes) because their tables were never created — they render only because of demo fallbacks.
4. The employee column is nearly empty across PRIMORA while competitors that win professionals (StyleSeat, Glamiva, SQUIRE) all treat the individual professional as a first-class user.
