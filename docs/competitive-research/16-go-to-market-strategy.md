# 16 — Go-to-Market Strategy

## 1. Positioning

**For Saudi salons and barbershops:** "Your clients stay yours — 0 % commission on them. We only charge when we bring you a new one, and it's less than half of what others take."
Arabic working line (HYPOTHESIS, copy to be tested): «عملاؤك لك — بدون عمولة. وندفع معك فقط لما نجيب لك عميل جديد.»

**For customers:** "Book your barber or salon in Arabic, in seconds, on WhatsApp — with real reviews, prayer-aware times and the ability to book for your family."

| Against | PRIMORA's message |
|---|---|
| Fresha (KSA) | No 50 % new-client fee; Arabic-first; WhatsApp reminders included |
| Glamera | We bring you clients, not just software; simpler, faster to start |
| SPOT | Same WhatsApp-first operations **plus** a marketplace that sends you new clients; public, transparent prices |
| Samha / Laha / Glamiva | Men's grooming and family booking; verified businesses; fair fees |

## 4. Customer acquisition

| Channel | How | Cost driver | Stage |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Provider-driven** (primary) | Each provider shares link/QR with existing clients; reminders and receipts carry the PRIMORA brand subtly | Free (provider's own audience) | From day 1 |
| WhatsApp | Booking link in shop's WhatsApp status and auto-reply; click-to-WhatsApp ads (72 h free messaging window, Meta) | Ad spend | Month 2+ |
| Local SEO | Server-rendered pages per district and service in Arabic ("حلاق في الملقا", "صالون نسائي في الياسمين") | Content + engineering | Month 6+ (needs G28) |
| Referral | Customer credit funded by PRIMORA for the first booking of a friend | Credit cost | Month 6+ |
| Seasonal campaigns | Ramadan late-night bookings; **pre-Eid "guaranteed slot"**; National Day; back-to-school haircuts; wedding season | Campaign spend | Ramadan 2027 (expected to begin around early February 2027, subject to moon sighting) |
| Family booking | "Book for your sons in one go" — father-and-son barbershop visits (SQUIRE benchmark) | Creative | Month 3+ |
| Licensed influencers | Local barbers / stylists with followers (Mawthooq licence) | Per campaign; prefer performance-based | Month 4+ |
| Google Maps / Business Profile | Booking link on each provider's profile | Free | Month 2+ |

**Do not** spend on broad consumer ads before district density is reached (section 5).

## 5. Marketplace launch strategy

1. **Supply first, district by district.** Consumer marketing in a district starts only when it has **≥ 15 live providers within ~5 km** with **≥ 60 % of their working hours bookable online** (HYPOTHESIS thresholds).
2. **Own-client bookings create the first liquidity.** Every provider brings existing clients through the link; this produces reviews, data and repeat usage before PRIMORA pays for demand.
3. **Then marketplace demand.** Customers acquired by PRIMORA see a dense choice set in their district.
4. **Measure liquidity per district:** search-to-booking rate, % of searches with ≥ 3 available slots in 48 h, provider fill rate.
5. **Expand to the adjacent district** only when the current one meets its gate.

## 6. WhatsApp-first operating model

| Moment | Message (utility template unless noted) | Cost (Meta third-party rate card) |
|---|---|---|
| Phone verification | Authentication OTP | ≈ SAR 0.04 |
| Booking confirmed | Confirmation with map link, policy summary, "add to calendar" | ≈ SAR 0.04 |
| 24 h before | Reminder with **Confirm / Reschedule / Cancel** buttons | ≈ SAR 0.04 |
| 2 h before | Short reminder | ≈ SAR 0.04 |
| After visit | Review request + "Book the same again" | ≈ SAR 0.04 |
| Owner / professional | New booking, cancellation, daily schedule at 9 am | ≈ SAR 0.04 each |
| Marketing (opt-in only) | Offers, seasonal campaigns | ≈ SAR 0.19 |
| Later | Arabic AI receptionist answering "is 9 pm free tomorrow?" and returning a pay link | Meta Business Agent $2 / 1M tokens (Meta docs) |

Per booking, the customer-side utility set costs ≈ **SAR 0.12–0.16**, versus Fresha KSA charging salons **SAR 0.15–0.30 per WhatsApp message**.

## 8. Go-to-market risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Glamera bundles marketplace + SaaS + licensed payments with SAR 3–4B processed and ~4,500 partners | Do not fight on ERP features; win on fair fees, WhatsApp, Arabic UX and demand in chosen districts |
| Fresha lowers its Saudi new-client fee | PRIMORA's own-client 0 % and local operations remain; keep fee cap flexible |
| Providers accept free months then leave | Measure value delivered (new clients, no-shows avoided); founding price lock only with annual commitment after month 6 |
| Demand fails to materialise | Go/no-go gate at month 6 (report 13); pivot option: SaaS + WhatsApp operations tool (SPOT-like) |
| Sales capacity for women's salons | Female sales lead before targeting the segment |
