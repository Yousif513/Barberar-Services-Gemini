**Subject:** VAT question before launch: is our booking marketplace the deemed supplier under amended VAT IR Art. 47(3)?

Dear [Adviser name],

We run **PRIMORA**, a Saudi online marketplace for beauty and grooming appointments: barbershops, salons, spas and home-service
professionals. We would like your written opinion on our VAT position before we launch.

**How the platform works**
- Customers book a provider's service on our website or app. The service price is set by the provider in our platform. We also run
  platform-funded promotions such as referral credit, loyalty points and coupons.
- The customer pays a deposit or the full amount online. Payment is processed by **Tap Payments** (a SAMA-licensed payment
  service provider). We intend to use Tap's Marketplace product so that Tap collects, splits and pays out, and PRIMORA itself never
  holds customer funds. [Confirm the current setup with Tap.]
- We handle customer complaints, cancellations and refunds through the platform.
- We earn a platform fee or commission per booking. Some providers will be VAT-registered and many small providers will be below
  the registration threshold.

**Our questions**
1. From **1 January 2026**, does amended **Article 47(3) of the VAT Implementing Regulations** make PRIMORA the **deemed supplier** of
   services provided through our platform by **resident providers who are not VAT-registered**? If so, must we charge 15% VAT on the
   **full booking price** for those bookings and issue the customer tax invoice in our own name?
2. For **VAT-registered providers**: can the provider be the supplier of record, with PRIMORA acting as a disclosed agent and charging
   VAT only on its own fee? What must the contracts, checkout and invoices show for that to hold?
3. How should **platform-funded rewards** (referral credit, loyalty points, coupons) be treated for VAT? Are they a price reduction on
   the booking, and does it matter whether PRIMORA or the provider is the supplier?
4. What does this mean for **ZATCA e-invoicing** (simplified tax invoices to consumers, invoices for our platform fees to
   providers)? Who must be onboarded to FATOORA?
5. What **record-retention period** applies to our tax invoices and accounting records? We are planning 10 years, based on the
   Commercial Books Law. Must the records be stored inside the Kingdom?
6. Is there any **VAT registration** or reporting step PRIMORA must complete before launch, based on our expected turnover
   [estimate: SAR ____ per year]?

**Sources we have seen (secondary summaries only, not the gazette text)**
- PwC: https://www.pwc.com/m1/en/services/tax/me-tax-legal-news/2025/approved-amendments-to-the-vat-implementing-regulations.html

We have built our system so that the invoicing path depends on each provider's VAT registration status, but we will not launch
until we have your answer. Please let us know your fee and timeline.

السادة [اسم المستشار] المحترمين،

نحن نُشغّل منصة **بريمورا (PRIMORA)**، وهي منصة سعودية إلكترونية لحجز خدمات التجميل والعناية: صالونات الحلاقة، ومراكز التجميل،
والسبا، والخدمات المنزلية. نرغب في الحصول على رأيكم المكتوب بشأن وضعنا في ضريبة القيمة المضافة قبل الإطلاق.

**آلية عمل المنصة**
- يحجز العميل خدمة لدى مقدّم الخدمة عبر موقعنا أو تطبيقنا، ويحدّد مقدّم الخدمة السعر داخل المنصة، وتقدّم المنصة عروضاً على
  حسابها (رصيد إحالة، ونقاط ولاء، وقسائم).

  ونعتزم استخدام منتج Marketplace لدى Tap بحيث تتولّى Tap التحصيل والتقسيم والتحويل، ولا تحتفظ المنصة بأموال العملاء.
  [يُؤكَّد الترتيب الحالي مع Tap.]

**أسئلتنا**
١. اعتباراً من **١ يناير ٢٠٢٦**، هل تجعل المادة **47(3)** المعدّلة من اللائحة التنفيذية منصتنا **المورد الحكمي** للخدمات

   وإصدار الفاتورة الضريبية للعميل باسمنا؟
٢. بالنسبة لمقدّمي الخدمة **المسجّلين**: هل يمكن أن يكون مقدّم الخدمة هو المورد، وتكون المنصة وكيلاً معلَناً تحتسب الضريبة على
   رسومها فقط؟ وما الذي يجب أن تتضمّنه العقود وصفحة الدفع والفواتير لذلك؟
٣. ما المعاملة الضريبية لـ **المكافآت التي تموّلها المنصة** (رصيد الإحالة، ونقاط الولاء، والقسائم)؟

   الربط مع منصة فاتورة؟

   داخل المملكة؟
