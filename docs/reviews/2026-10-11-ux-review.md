# UX review round 1: governance, money and privacy screens (2026-10-11)

Reviewer: `ux-round1-claude` (adminwright ux-reviewer). Branch `claude-code`, `primora-fix`. No code, migration or server
change was made. Findings are filed as manifest gaps with the prefix `ux1-`. Evidence:
`.admin-console/evidence/ux-round1/` (`session-log.txt`, one crawl JSON per role/language/width, about 80 screenshots).

## Verdict

Operators can do the core governance work. They can sign in with TOTP, propose a threshold change, take it through
step-up and get it approved by a second administrator. Both runs were done by keyboard only, one of them in Arabic at
375 px. Privacy requests can be raised by a customer and worked by an owner. Three things get in the way:

- The provider pricing page contradicts the real fee.
- Reconciliation shows two different truths in two places.
- A privacy request that arrives by e-mail or phone cannot be logged, because the form needs a customer UUID that no
  screen displays.

Finance also meets owner-only screens and controls everywhere, with a forbidden message that tells them to ask for the
wrong thing.

## How it was checked

- **Tooling.** Playwright 1.61.1 (Chromium 149), isolated contexts, real sign-in. The owner and finance accounts
  enrolled TOTP through `/login/mfa`, and codes were computed from the shown key. The provider owner and the smoke
  customer use the passwords from the smoke fixture. The customer's e-mail code was read from Mailpit.
- **Crawl.** 15 admin routes:
  - as owner in EN at 1440 px and in AR at 375 px;
  - as finance in EN at 1440 px.

  For each route the crawl recorded console errors, 4xx responses, page overflow, h1, alerts and a digit census.
- **Flows by keyboard.**
  - MFA enrolment and challenge, EN and AR.
  - Threshold proposal with step-up (owner, EN, desktop).
  - Approve with step-up (finance, AR, 375 x 600). The change was reverted afterwards the same way.
  - Privacy detail, extension and log forms, and the propose dialog (EN and AR, 375 px).
  - Customer privacy request through the code step (AR, 375 px).
  - Dialog open and Escape on role removal, clear profile, fee proposal and settings programmes.
  - Reconciliation commands and import.
  - Audit log and customer filtered-empty states.
- **Dialog geometry.** Dialogs were measured at 375 x 600: position, containing ancestors, and whether the primary
  action can be reached.
- **Data this pass created.**
  - One "access" privacy request for `smoke.customer.1791700651579@example.test`.
  - Two approved threshold changes. The net value is unchanged: `refund_daily_cumulative_sar` is still 5000.00.
  - One reconciliation run for 2026-10-10.
  - Verified TOTP factors for `admin.owner@primora.local` and `admin.finance@primora.local`.

## What works

- **Sign-in.** MFA enrolment and challenge in both languages. The code field gets focus on load, there is no overflow
  at 375 px, and the format error is localized.
- **Command dialogs and step-up.** Focus is trapped inside the dialog, Escape returns focus to the trigger, and input is
  kept on a validation failure. A refused command reopens as the step-up prompt and, after the code, completes and shows
  "waiting for a second administrator".
- **Approve flow.** Works by keyboard in Arabic RTL at 375 px, and both dialogs keep their actions reachable.
- **Customer privacy request.** Works by keyboard in Arabic: the radio group, then the code step (focus moves to "Send
  code"), then the request is listed.
- **States.**
  - Filtered-empty states are correct on the audit log and on customers.
  - The forbidden state is rendered, not blank, for finance.
  - Owners see no console errors on the 15 crawled routes in either language.
  - No page-level horizontal overflow at 375 px.
- **Destructive dialogs.** "Clear profile details" previews what is removed and kept, and requires a typed confirmation
  and a reason.

## Console errors seen

- **Owner:** none on the 15 routes. Additional errors:
  - `/admin/privacy`: invalid pattern regex.
  - `/login/mfa`: 404 on factor cleanup, and 422 on a wrong code (expected).
  - Expected 403s before step-up: `admin_request_setting_change` and `admin_decide_approval`.
  - `/admin/reconciliation`: 503 from `reconcile-psp` (Tap not configured locally).
- **Finance:** 403s on `/admin` (`admin_list_audit_events` x2), `/admin/customers` (16 errors), `/admin/audit-logs`,
  `/admin/privacy` and `/admin/incidents`.
- **Customer:** 403 on `privacy_submit_request` (the designed first attempt), and 403 on `/auth/v1/verify` for a wrong
  code.

## Not verified

- Loading and error states, which cannot be triggered from the UI without breaking the stack.
- Payout list and IBAN reveal (no data).
- A screen-reader pass.
- Contrast sweeps.
- `/admin/incidents` and `/admin/settings` save flows beyond opening them.
- `/provider/pricing` and `/become-provider` at 375 px in English.

`/admin/privacy` was checked while a fix agent was changing privacy server code. No route returned an error caused by a
migration.
