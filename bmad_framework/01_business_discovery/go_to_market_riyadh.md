# Riyadh Go-To-Market & Validation Strategy

To address the marketplace "chicken-and-egg" challenge, we will execute a hyper-local launch starting in high-density, high-income districts of Riyadh (**Al-Malqa, Al-Yasmin, Al-Olaya, and Al-Sulaimaniyah**).

---

## 1. Validation Strategy (Pre-Launch)

Before fully launching, we must validate both supply and demand hypotheses:

### Phase A: Supply-Side Validation (The "Concierge" Test)
* **Objective**: Validate if Salons and Freelancers are willing to trust a third-party platform for calendar management.
* **Execution**: Manually pitch to 30 high-end salons and 20 freelance makeup artists in Riyadh. Offer them a "Free-for-life onboarding concierge package" (professional photography, catalog structuring, and 3 months of free Pro features).
* **Success Metric**: Secure signed Letters of Intent (LOI) or pre-registrations from at least 15 reputable salons and 10 freelancers.

### Phase B: Demand-Side Validation (The Landing Page Test)
* **Objective**: Validate client interest in booking beauty/grooming services online.
* **Execution**: Deploy a landing page with mock booking flows for the top 5 salons in Riyadh. Run localized Snapchat and TikTok ads targeting Riyadh residents (ages 18-35).
* **Success Metric**: Capture 1,000+ early-access email/phone registrations with a click-through rate (CTR) on booking buttons above 4.5%.

---

## 2. Provider Recruitment Strategy (Supply Acquisition)

To build a high-quality catalog that naturally attracts customers, we must onboard the supply side first:

1. **The "Free Migration" Offer**: 
   - Directly target salons using manual paper calendars or basic WhatsApp business accounts.
   - Offer manual migration services: our team physically visits the salon to input their staff details, schedules, and service catalog into the platform.
2. **Professional Media Kit**:
   - Provide a professional photographer to take high-quality pictures of the salon interior, staff, and past work. This populates their portfolios and elevates the platform's visual standard.
3. **Freelancer Licensing Support**:
   - Assist freelance service providers in getting their official freelance documents from the Ministry of Human Resources (MHRSD) or Balady municipality, making them legal operators on our marketplace.
4. **B2B Referral Incentives**:
   - Offer existing salons 500 SAR in platform credits (for ads/boosts) for every other salon they refer that joins and completes 20 bookings.

---

## 3. Customer Acquisition Strategy (Demand Acquisition)

Once we have 25+ verified salons and 30+ freelance service providers live in Riyadh, we launch consumer campaigns:

### A. Localized Influencer Marketing (The Riyadh Channel)
* **Snapchat & TikTok**: These are the primary social platforms in Saudi Arabia. We will collaborate with micro-influencers (10k-50k followers) based in Riyadh.
* **Promo Codes**: Provide influencers with unique codes (e.g., `RIYADH15`) offering 15% off their first booking, subsidized by the platform to kickstart transaction velocity.

### B. Geofenced Social Media Campaigns
* Run targeted Instagram and Snapchat stories geofenced strictly to a 3km radius around partner salons.
* *Example Ad Copy*: "Stuck waiting at the salon? Book your slot at [Salon Name] in Al-Malqa now and walk in exactly on time."

### C. Double-Sided Referral Program
* A "Give 20 SAR, Get 20 SAR" referral program. When a user refers a friend, both receive 20 SAR in platform wallet credits upon completion of the friend's first booking.

### D. Corporate Partnerships
* Partner with large Riyadh-based tech hubs and corporations (e.g., STC, Elm, Riyadh Combat) to offer exclusive employee discounts on home styling and grooming services.
