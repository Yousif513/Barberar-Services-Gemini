# CEO collaboration preferences

Recorded 23 October 2026 from the CEO's explicit request.

Every future next-step response must state the next action, provide its ready-to-send prompt, and recommend a model and reasoning effort for that particular action. Use currently available model/effort names; distinguish a recommendation from the model actually running. Do not automatically switch model settings or purchase services. Explain a material change in recommendation briefly. Preserve all existing approval boundaries.

This preference is guidance for communicating about development, not an employee activation, business permission or execution authorization.
