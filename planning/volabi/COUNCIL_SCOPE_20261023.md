## Lifecycle, decisions and evidence

Proposed local preparation states: draft -> awaiting preparation decision -> preparation confirmed locally, or changes requested/declined; cancellation preserves history. Notes/minutes have a separate draft -> awaiting minutes decision -> confirmed minutes record lifecycle, with append-only corrected versions. No state says an actual meeting happened merely because its time passed or its brief was approved.

A founder may enter a reported session time or observation as an explicit attributed claim. The interface distinguishes planned time, reported session and verified evidence; attendance stays unknown unless actual evidence is provided. Inactive AI identities can only be proposed contributors. The first slice neither validates an external meeting nor marks AI participation as observed.

Preparation approval and minutes approval are separate exact existing task/recommendation/decision packets. Bind actor/workspace, meeting revision, agenda/participant/time/purpose and source versions/hashes, with checked command receipts and audit. For a founder-originated brief, a real versioned founder-authored source may ground the request; label it as founder intent, not independent corroboration. Keep this first slice within the existing twelve-source decision boundary; refuse overflow with retained input.

Material changes invalidate pending preparation/minutes packets and visibly supersede their current acceptance. A minutes correction names its affected minutes snapshot and linked dependent handoffs explicitly; it does not invalidate unrelated preparation packets or unrelated tasks. Cancelled records reject new preparation/minutes submissions and action handoffs; earlier notes, decisions and receipts remain readable. Reopening requires an explicit reasoned revision and a fresh applicable confirmation. Preserve historical decisions, approved minutes versions and dissent. Follow-up must revalidate current material; unrelated minutes corrections cannot silently authorize old tasks. A task or project acceptance remains subject to its existing rules; a meeting mention does not close it. Approval records discussion/decision separately from execution authorization. Spoken 'approved' and AI agreement cannot substitute for an authenticated exact decision.

Atomic local commands cover create/revise/cancel, participant/material changes, preparation submission, notes/minutes submission/correction and action links. Use the existing session, CSRF, row/object scope, transactional versions, audit and same-command receipts. An audit/receipt failure rolls back the entire operation. Preserve drafts on network failure and recover the existing command before resubmission. Additive local records only; production identity, recovery and retention remain separate prerequisites.
