# primora-refund-case-review

Start from the actual case, booking/purchase, customer and salon scope; the confirmed customer terms and applicable policy version; payment/capture/refund evidence; service/cancellation facts; and any exact approval record. Missing evidence is unknown, not evidence against either party. Separate a customer statement, provider statement, system event and independently checked fact.

Prepare an eligibility/remedy recommendation only under the adopted policy. Preserve customer choice where an alternative booking or credit requires it. Contested remedies need impartial escalation/appeal under the actual policy; an AI persona cannot unilaterally resolve disputed rights or invent compensation limits. Emma/Lina can contextualize the case, Sarah can contribute operational evidence, Daniel can check financial impact, Omar can independently assure and Layla can identify legal/compliance questions. These are candidate handoffs, not new appointments, permissions or mandatory approval counts.

Bind any proposed refund to immutable target/capture, currency, minor-unit amount, reason, policy/terms revision, recipient/source allocation, approval validity and authorized operation. Yesterday's approval for booking A / SAR150 cannot be applied to booking B / SAR200. Changed material requires its applicable new decision; do not reuse an old approval or silently split a larger amount into smaller requests.

Keep eligibility/recommendation, decision approval, execution authorization, submitted, unknown, rejected, partially refunded, verified refunded and disputed/appealed separate. A submission timeout does not mean failed or refunded. Preserve the original operation/reference and request evidence; observe its actual result before considering another dispatch. Only the provider's checked result plus the matching accounting/customer record establishes the relevant completed state. A completed refund is not the same as a completed service or a new acquisition event.

Apply the founder's no-custody/direct-provider allocation requirement. Do not assume Primora has salon funds available to fund a remedy; record actual provider-supported reversal/debit arrangements, fee treatment and unresolved recovery obligations. Apply the actual adopted financial-control source before calling a decision missing: Primora repository Q5/Q8/Q9/D3 are conditionally adopted subject to their exact prerequisites. Q9 distinguishes Tap money movement from ledger entitlements and approved reconciliation adjustments; preserve that authority split when applicable. Do not silently override financial history, ignore an unmet prerequisite, use another tenant's balance or invent a tax/legal conclusion.

Return a case packet with attributed chronology, evidence ids/hashes/as-of, eligible options and rationale, exact proposed material, missing policy/business decisions, independent appeal route and truthful customer-language status draft. No gateway call, money movement, policy adoption or customer message is performed by preparing this packet. Read [references/source-and-cases.md](references/source-and-cases.md) for the original limits and realistic evaluation cases.
