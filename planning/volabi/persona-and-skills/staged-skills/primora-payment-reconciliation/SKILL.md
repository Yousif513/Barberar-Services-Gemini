# primora-payment-reconciliation

Use the actual schema/contract and authorized evidence scope. Preserve original receipts and immutable references; record provider/environment, merchant/salon, customer/purchase, operation/charge/attempt ids, amount/currency, timestamps, event type and source version/hash. A signed callback, authenticated refetch, settlement statement and bank entry are different evidence categories. No local ledger row, success flag, image, estimate or test fixture alone proves funds were allocated or settled.

Match customer payment -> provider capture -> allocation entitlements -> refunds/reversals/chargebacks -> settlement/bank evidence as separate stages. Validate target binding and currency/amount before joining records; do not match only on an equal amount or a display name. Explain duplicates, missing/out-of-order records, partial outcomes, unknown submissions and aging with their actual as-of. Use current provider primary documentation and the actual approved merchant contract for status/lookup behavior when integrating; do not infer timeout retry safety or absence from an empty lookup.

Preserve the founder's first-acquisition-only customer-salon model and zero repeat acquisition commission. The exact qualifying/earning event remains unadopted unless a current adopted commercial source establishes it: do not promote the historical assistant recommendation of completed-service eligibility into approved policy. Keep service/completion evidence, any proposed earning trigger and allocation at original checkout separate; reserving/allocating a fee does not prove it is earned. Link the relationship identity, original attribution/qualifying completion, rule version and concurrency/reversal history. No deletion/recreation or refunded event silently resets acquisition history. Where the exact relationship boundary, existing-customer treatment or reversal policy is not adopted, report the decision gap rather than selecting a rate or policy.

For allocation, prove provider-supported non-custodial assignment to the actual licensed merchant arrangement; an internal split, routing flag or application ledger is insufficient. Distinguish allocation time from bank-credit time. Trace every minor unit through the contract's actual gross/net/fee/tax/adjustment categories without double-counting VAT. Processing fees depend on provider, underlying method and the salon agreement; unknown actual fee stays unknown, and an estimate remains labelled. Do not use a universal3percent fee or treat an unexplained SAR3 as Primora revenue. Apply only adopted rounding/tolerance rules; no arbitrary balancing plug.

Return a read-only reconciliation with matched/explained/exception states, control totals, source ids and evidence quality, expected-vs-actual deductions, acquisition eligibility and unresolved policy/authority facts. Propose a bounded corrective action for separate approval; do not post journals, rewrite history, fund refunds, change routing/destination or send statements. Apply the current adopted authority contract: in the Primora repository, Q9 conditionally makes Tap authoritative for money movement and the ledger for entitlements, with approved reconciliation adjustments. Check its exact counsel/external prerequisites; when a prerequisite is unmet, name that unmet condition rather than calling Q9 wholly unadopted. A provider/ledger/bank disagreement remains an exception until the applicable evidence matching and authorized reconciliation adjustment have their checked receipts. Mask unrelated PII/IBANs and keep private wire/credential data outside ordinary report output.

Read [references/source-and-cases.md](references/source-and-cases.md) for the source constraints and discrimination against existing finance/pricing/payment skills.
